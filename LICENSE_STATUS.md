# Licensing and ownership

The maintainer has not selected a general open-source license for original
project code yet. Public visibility is not a blanket reuse or relicensing
grant. Contact Arka Roy Chowdhury for licensing questions; this status will be
updated when a license is selected. Preserve upstream notices in the meantime.

Magisk installer code retains its upstream GPLv3 license; the corresponding
source and license are in third_party/magisk. The Gradle wrapper retains its
Apache-2.0 notices. A future original-code license will not relicense those
components or proprietary OEM firmware.

The realme/OPPO APK, framework, camera ELF files, models and proprietary SDKs
retain their owners' rights. The port does not claim ownership of their
algorithms or publish their source. Credits, hashes, an unchanged binary and a
public vendor tree do not by themselves establish a general redistribution
license. Do not assume this repository grants permission to rehost OEM files.
For a source-only workflow, supply firmware you are entitled to use; extraction
and full clean-clone packaging remain documented build limitations.
